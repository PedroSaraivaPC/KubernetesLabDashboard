Scale a Deployment

A Deployment manages a desired number of Pod replicas through its ReplicaSet. Choose a KubeLab Deployment and a count from 1 to 6. Scaling up creates Pods; scaling down removes Pods.

Replace a Pod

Delete one Pod belonging to a KubeLab Deployment. Its ReplicaSet creates a new Pod to maintain the desired replica count. The new Pod has a new UID and may run on a different worker. This does not change the desired count.

The backend verifies that the Pod belongs to an allowed Deployment. Its controller creates the replacement after deletion.
